Our proposal for [XRWay’26: XR on the Way – XR for Walking, Driving, and Everyday Mobility](https://xrontheway.github.io/) has been accepted as an IEEE ISMAR 2026 workshop. The workshop will be organized by researchers from Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology (GIST, Republic of Korea), Korea Advanced Institute of Science & Technology (KAIST, Republic of Korea), Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT, United States), and Toyota Research Institute (TRI, United States).
