I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology (GIST). Concurrently, I serve as a postdoctoral researcher for the DGIST InnoCORE Research Group, focusing on Bio-Embodied Physical AI. Prior to my current position, I served as a Research Assistant Professor in the department of AI convergence at GIST for one year. I received my Ph.D. in Integrated Technology (specializing in Intelligent Robotics) from GIST in 2023. Before that, I earned my M.S. in Mechatronics Engineering from GIST in 2017. My research lies at the intersection of Human Computer/Robot Interaction, Extended Reality, and Physical AI.
